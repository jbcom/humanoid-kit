# Sculpted sources of the adult anatomy pack

The adult pack's sculpt transfers currently use CC0 community sources. A
CC-BY 4.0 anatomical model is recorded for the planned female hood/glans transfer
(docs/research/ADULT-SCULPT-PLAN.md, sections 6d, 6e and 6f). This directory holds
the records of the community downloads and of their cuts, and no geometry: the
`.obj` and `.proxy` files and the cuts derived from them are not in the repository
(owner ruling, 2026-10-17). The table also identifies the NIH download whose cuts
have not been retained. This directory is not part of the published package
(`packs/adult-anatomy/package.json` ships `data/` only), and nothing the public site
builds from reads it.

## How the packer gets the geometry

`scripts/lib/detail/sculptSources.ts` (run by `pnpm pack:data`, by the tests that
place the sculpts, and by `pnpm pack:adult-sources`) downloads each file from the URL
in its source's `LICENSE-SOURCE.txt` into a gitignored cache
(`.cache/adult-sculpts/`, or `$HK_ADULT_SOURCE_CACHE`) and checks its SHA-256 against
the table below. It then regenerates the cuts from the verified `.obj` with
`scripts/blender/cut_sculpt.py` (headless Blender, run by the packer; it finds
`$BLENDER`, `blender` on the PATH or a standard install) and checks each cut's SHA-256
and the whole of `cuts.json` against the committed record. It fails closed: a failed
download, a hash that differs from the one recorded here, a missing Blender or a cut
that comes out differently stops the run with the file named, and no unverified file
is ever read. The cuts in `cuts.json` were regenerated byte for byte with Blender
5.1.1. The geometry is never committed; the URLs and hashes are the whole record of it.

The licence rule's clause B governs: an asset's page licence is its licence
(owner ruling, 2026-10-09). Each source below was judged from its page. An upload
whose page says CC0 is CC0 even where it was derived from an AGPL parent (owner
ruling, 2026-10-10: "It isn't on us to make determinations of the validity of a
derived product"); a parent is recorded as history, not as a licence condition.

## How each file is used

- `adult_male_genitalia_breast_fix.obj`: a whole-body proxy in metres with
  flaccid genitals. `scripts/blender/cut_sculpt.py` subdivides it twice and cuts
  out the hanging shaft with its glans (`phallus.obj`, cut across the shaft's
  own axis a centimetre below the root) and the sac (`scrotum.obj`, cut just
  below its neck: the sac hangs free, with two lobes and a median raphe).
- `Male_Gen-Heal1.obj`: a whole-body proxy in metres with an erect penis. The
  same script cuts out the erect shaft with its glans (`phallus.obj`).
- In each directory, `cuts.json` holds the cut's planes, the source's hash, each
  part's topology numbers and the SHA-256 of the part as the cut script writes it
  (`objSha256`). The packer refuses a source that no longer has its hash here, and
  a regenerated part that does not have its `objSha256`.
- Each `.proxy`: the asset's own binding. It is read only to place the sculpt on
  our base: it is evaluated on our rest body, and a per-axis scale and
  translation is fitted to it (`scripts/lib/detail/sculpt.ts`). The parts are
  then bound to our base by our own regenerated binding. No part of these files
  ships. The erect shaft is then moved, without turning, so its cut is centred on
  the flaccid shaft's: the two authors put the root in different places, and one
  organ has one root.
- `adult_female_2020_v_01.obj`: a whole-body proxy in decimetres with a vulva:
  majora, minora, a cleft and a shallow vestibule, resolved once subdivided twice;
  its hood and glans are one rod. It is placed on our base by a similarity fitted
  to where its own proxy's reference vertices put each vertex (the source's units
  come out of the fit), and the majora and minora are transferred from it onto the
  skin as detail targets (ADULT-SCULPT-PLAN.md, 6f).
- NIH 3D `3DPX-017321`: a closed anatomical sculpt of a female pelvis with the
  perineal structures, in its own units. The download was recovered in the prior
  session's private scratch directory, but no NIH cuts, `cuts.json`, landmark fit
  or transfer implementation are retained here as of the 2026-10-11 recovery.
  Its proposed use for the clitoral glans, hood separation and vestibule depth
  remains planned. The reported licence is CC-BY 4.0, so the pack, which is CC0
  1.0, ships nothing derived from it; using it needs an owner ruling on shipping
  CC BY data in the adult pack first.
- No material (`.mhmat`) or weights (`.mhw`) file is used or kept.

## Not used, and why

- ukiyoe's `man_genital` (<http://www.makehumancommunity.org/clothes/man_genital.html>,
  CC0) was the first source. It is a clothes piece whose sac is merged with the
  skin round it, and whose glans is a plain cap; the two proxies above have a free
  two-lobed sac and a glans with its corona and meatus, and between them a flaccid
  and an erect form, so the organ is drawn from sculpts in both states rather than
  posed from one (owner ruling, 2026-10-09: no procedural forms).
- xsuprem3x's `adult_male_genitalia` (adult_male_genitalia.html_0) is the same
  body as ieroglif's fixed version above, which corrects its chest.
