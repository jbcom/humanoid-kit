# Channels behind the body's apertures

The numbers `src/affordance/channel.ts` draws an aperture's channel from: how
deep it runs behind its rim and how wide it is along the way. As in
`SKIN-STATES.md`, each number is either **measured** (read from a cited
source) or a **CHOICE** (modelled where no measurement was found, and labelled
so here and in the code). Sources were read 2026-10-09.

A channel is straight along its rim's inward normal. Real channels curve (the
ear canal bends twice, the mouth turns down into the throat), but a straight
path carries what a developer needs first: what is inside and how far in, and
where to hide what has passed the rim. The curved paths are a later step.

Every dimension below is an adult's and is scaled to the figure by the width of
its head (the distance between its ear canals over the default adult's), so a
child's channels are a child's size.

## The ear canal (the external auditory canal)

- **Entry**: 7.75 ± 1 mm high and 6.1 ± 0.8 mm wide; **isthmus** (the narrowest
  point) 6.8 ± 0.97 by 5.2 ± 0.76 mm. CT of 100 temporal bones (200 sides),
  El-Anwar et al., Int Arch Otorhinolaryngol 2023,
  <https://thieme-connect.de/products/ejournals/html/10.1055/s-0042-1758213>.
  **Measured.**
- **Length**: 2 to 3 cm in adults, of which the outer third (about 8 mm) is
  cartilage and the inner two thirds (about 16 mm) bone (the same article's
  review; clinical references agree). The canal is drawn 24 mm deep, the two
  segments' sum. **Measured** (a range; the value taken is its documented
  segment sum).
- The isthmus is placed at the junction of the two segments, a third of the
  way in. **CHOICE**: the sources give its size, not its depth.

## The nostril (the nasal vestibule)

- **Nostril floor width**: 10.98 ± 1.40 mm (men), 10.13 ± 1.73 mm (women),
  Korean adults in their twenties,
  <https://www.koreascience.or.kr/article/JAKO200907642268558.page?&lang=en>;
  11.00 mm, young Turkish men. **Measured.** The rim's width across is taken as
  10.5 mm, between the two.
- **Nostril axis**: about 60% of the alar length in a 3D study of 20 young
  white adults (the same review), against an alar length of 24.7 to 27.1 mm
  (Korean) gives an axis of about 15 mm: the rim's long diameter. **Measured**
  (derived from two measured ratios).
- **Depth**: no measured vestibule depth was found. The channel is drawn 15 mm
  deep, the vestibule only, ending where the nasal valve narrows. **CHOICE.**
- **Direction**: the vestibule rises from the nostril and turns back into the
  nasal cavity; the skin's own inward normal at the nostril's floor points up
  and a little forward, out of the head. The straight channel runs midway
  between that normal and the way to the head's centre (midway between the ear
  canals). **CHOICE**; a test holds every channel's far end nearer the head's
  centre than its rim.

## The mouth (the oral cavity)

- **Depth**: no direct incisor-to-oropharynx distance was found. The
  oropharyngeal airways used to hold an adult's airway open are sized from the
  incisors to the angle of the jaw and come in 40 to 120 mm (ISO 5364:2016);
  the adult sizes are 80 to 100 mm. The channel is drawn 90 mm deep, the middle
  adult size, as a proxy for the distance from the lips to the back of the
  tongue. **CHOICE** (on a measured proxy).
- **Width at the rim**: the opening's width is the figure's own mouth (the
  distance between the corners of the lips), not a number here.
- **How far it opens**: the channel's height at the rim runs from 0 (closed) to
  the full opening; the full opening is drawn as 45 mm between the lips.
  **CHOICE**: no measured maximal opening was read for this note.
- Inside, the channel keeps its rim's size to half its depth and narrows to
  60% at the back. **CHOICE.**
